# Monroe 1887 pilot: Clarkson, DIST. N°. 4

Open `index.html` directly from an extracted folder. No remote code, basemap, account or runtime query is required. The application is separate from the Common School Archive statewide atlas.

## One mapmaker's depiction

J. B. Beers & Co., *Map of Monroe County, New York* (1887), [LOC item 2013593279](https://www.loc.gov/item/2013593279/), [resource g3803m.la000520](https://www.loc.gov/resource/g3803m.la000520/). Credit: **Library of Congress, Geography and Map Division**. Item/API metadata specifies that digitized map collections are free to use and reuse unless a Rights Advisory indicates otherwise; no item-specific restriction appeared in the checked resource JSON. The historical sheet bears an 1887 copyright imprint, not a current rights warning. The included cropped raster is credited and not relicensed under the code license. No modern third-party restoration was used.

I inspected the printed title and place-symbol index, the full raster overview and a native-resolution crop. The printed key explains geographic index letters/numbers and place symbols (post offices, telegraph etc.); it does **not** explain school shading in the inspected key. The LOC catalog independently describes numbered districts and hand coloring. On the Clarkson crop, the distinct colored block and dashed edge delimit the label written **DIST. N°. 4**. A different **SCHOOL N°. 4** label marks a building and was not used as a boundary. Solid survey-section lines and diagonal Rochester distance arcs are not district boundaries. The block is on one sheet remote from the visible folds, and no district line is joined across a seam. The top traced side follows the outside of the highlighted district where it meets the printed Clarkson–Hamlin line. This is a one-district cartographic reading, not proof of the legally operative boundary.

The cropped native raster is supplied from the [LOC IIIF pixel region](https://tile.loc.gov/image-services/iiif/service:gmd:gmd380:g3803:g3803m:la000520/345,3660,1400,1420/1400,/0/default.jpg). The [quarter-scale source](https://tile.loc.gov/image-services/iiif/service:gmd:gmd380:g3803:g3803m:la000520/full/pct:25/0/default.jpg) was used for the historic control frame, origin 87,887 in that downloaded JPEG, and preserved here as `data/loc-clarkson-control-frame.png`. The four manually marked corners and their native pixel frame are stored in `data/native-trace.json`. Pixel corner placement is interpretive, to a few historic scan pixels; image registration dominates the measured error.

## Pairing, fit and check

Control features pair clearly identifiable road crossings on the historical raster with modern centerline intersections from [NYS ITS Streets, layer 4](https://nysgeohub.ny.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Transportation/NYS_Streets/MapServer/4), plus one counterpart of the printed NW Clarkson township/county-line junction from the [U.S. Census 2024 cartographic county-subdivision shapefile](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2024/shp/cb_2024_36_cousub_500k.zip). The modern centerline query is saved under `data/ny-streets-query.txt`; the reduced Census geometry is reference only. Continuity between depicted and modern crossings is a match hypothesis, not independent proof of unchanged street alignment.

A local 2D affine fit uses five named pairs. All four traced corners fall within their historic pixel convex hull. Three paired crossings were withheld from fitting and evaluated afterward; the checks are model-independent, not independent surveys. Geographic differences are expressed in local-scaled degrees (81,152 m/degree longitude, 111,110 m/degree latitude) around 43.25° N. Model fit RMS: **21.6 m**. Withheld RMS: **164.4 m**; maximum **204.1 m**. Positional errors combine hand reading, a simple model, original distortion and possible road changes. The largest observed check is not an upper bound, standard error, confidence interval or uniform accuracy guarantee. No legal or cadastral precision should be inferred, no county-area calculations are warranted, and no meeting site has been georeferenced from this record.

## Rebuild and verify

Use Python 3 and NumPy: `python tools/build.py` rebuilds the same GeoJSON and JavaScript bundle from source-frame corners and the eight paired controls. Use `sha256sum -c DATA-SHA256SUMS` to check the data files; `node --check pilot.js` checks syntax. Browser tests: Playwright Python with Chromium, optional `CHROMIUM_BIN` override, `python tests/browser.py`. The field notes and the 1887 published map date are distinct from any creation date of the school district. MIT applies to the original app code; Leaflet retains its upstream license. No Newberry data are included.
